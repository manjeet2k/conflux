Overview

The Conflux browser extension is a companion to the free and open-source Conflux Download Accelerator desktop app. Its sole purpose is to send download requests from your browser to your local Conflux client.

Information we access

When you start a download with Conflux (the right-click "Download with Conflux" menu, or automatic download interception), the extension reads only what it needs to fulfil that request:

  • Target URL: the address of the file being downloaded.
  • Referrer URL: the Referer of the originating page, which some servers require to accept the request.
  • User-Agent: your browser's User-Agent string, to match what the server expects.
  • File name and size: the suggested filename and byte length from the browser's download event.

The extension requests no host permissions. It does not read page content, cookies, or your tabs.

Where your data goes

Your data never leaves your computer. It is handed to the Conflux desktop app over the operating system's standard input and output pipes (the WebExtensions Native Messaging API).

  • We operate no remote servers, cloud backends, or third-party proxies.
  • No browsing activity, URLs, or download logs are collected, transmitted, sold, or shared with anyone.

Data storage

Your extension settings (auto-interception on or off, file extension filters, excluded domains) are kept in browser extension storage (chrome.storage.sync). Download URLs are never written to persistent storage by the extension.

Analytics and third-party code

The extension contains no tracking scripts, advertising networks, or telemetry. It talks to no external domains, only to the native messaging host registered on your device.

Open source

Conflux is open source under the MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses. The desktop app and this extension are publicly verifiable at {{REPO_URL}}.
